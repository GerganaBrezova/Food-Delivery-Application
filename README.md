Food Delivery Application

The Food Delivery application is a modern solution for managing food delivery businesses, designed for interaction with restaurants and food producers. It offers features for managing orders, restaurant and product catalogs, and organizing deliveries. The application ensures efficient business operations through precise management and software tools.

------

Key Features:

- User Authentication: Login and logout functionalities for customers, employees, and delivery personnel with role-based access control.
- Registration: New customers and delivery staff can register within the system.
- Restaurant Management: Employees can add, edit, or delete restaurants and associated products.
- Delivery Management: Delivery staff can accept and fulfill delivery orders on a first-come-first-serve basis. Accepted deliveries cannot be taken by other couriers.
- Delivery Status Tracking: Real-time tracking of delivery status for customers.
- Revenue Reports: Employees can generate reports on the company’s turnover for a specified time period.
- Courier Revenue Tracking: Managers can generate reports on the earnings of each courier over a given time period.
- Courier Bonuses: Automatic bonus system for couriers who generate certain amounts of revenue, as defined in system settings.

------

Technologies Used

- Spring Boot: For backend development and handling business logic.
- Thymeleaf: For rendering dynamic HTML content.
- JPA (Java Persistence API): For database interaction.
- Spring Security: For managing user authentication and authorization.
- MySQL: For database management.
- Lombok: For reducing boilerplate code.

------

Usage

- Login: Users can log in as customers, employees, or delivery staff, with each role having specific permissions.
- Customer: Browse the food menu, filter by categories, and place orders.
- Employee: Manage restaurants, products, and view company turnover reports.
- Courier: Accept and complete delivery orders, change delivery status.
